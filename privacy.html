🔒 DreamDrop - Privacy Policy

This privacy policy explains how the "DreamDrop" dream interpretation app collects, uses, and protects your personal data.

🛡️ Important Note: Your dream data is stored only on your device and is not shared with third parties.

📊 Data We Collect

DreamDrop collects the following data:

  • Dream Texts: Dream descriptions you enter for interpretation
  • Usage Data: Anonymous usage statistics of the app
  • Device Information: Basic device info for app performance
  • Token Info: Purchase history and token balance

🔐 Data Storage

How your data is stored:

  • All dream data is stored locally on your device
  • No personal dream data is stored on servers
  • Local storage is managed using AsyncStorage
  • All data is deleted when the app is uninstalled

🤖 AI and Gemini API

For dream interpretations:

  • Dream texts are sent to Google Gemini API
  • Used solely for interpretation purposes
  • Subject to Google’s privacy policy
  • No personal identifying information is shared

💰 Purchase Information

In-app purchases:

  • Processed via Apple App Store / Google Play Store
  • Payment info is secured by the store
  • Only the purchase status is tracked
  • Credit card information is never stored

📱 App Permissions

DreamDrop requests the following permissions:

  • Storage: To save dream data
  • Internet: To receive AI-generated interpretations
  • Sharing: To share dream interpretations

🔄 Data Sharing

Your data:

  • Is never sold to third parties
  • Is not used for marketing purposes
  • Is only processed for technical requirements
  • Anonymous statistics may be collected

👤 User Rights

Your rights:

  • You can delete your data at any time
  • You can clear your history from the app settings
  • You can request account deletion
  • You can inquire about data usage

🛡️ Security

To protect your data:

  • Local encryption is used
  • Secure API connections (HTTPS)
  • Regular security updates
  • Minimal data collection principle

👶 Children's Privacy

DreamDrop is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

🔄 Policy Changes

This privacy policy may be updated. Significant changes will be announced in the app.
